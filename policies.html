Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: August 2026

We ship both within India and internationally, worldwide.

  • In-stock items are dispatched within 4-5 business days of your order being confirmed (i.e. after successful payment).
  • Pre-order items are sourced from our wholesaler contacts after your order is placed, so delivery takes longer than in-stock items — typically 15 business days for dispatch. Each pre-order product is clearly marked as such on its product page.
  • Delivery time after dispatch depends on your location and the delivery/courier partner handling your shipment, and is separate from the dispatch time above — domestic deliveries are typically faster than international ones.
  • International orders: any customs duties, import taxes, or other charges levied by your country's customs authority are the customer's responsibility and are not included in the order price. Delivery timelines for international orders can vary significantly depending on customs processing in the destination country, which is outside our control.
  • You'll receive your Order ID at checkout and in your email receipt — quote this if you contact us about your order's status.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Because many of our products are handmade or sourced in limited quantities, we do not accept returns or replacements for change of mind. Refunds are considered only in the specific cases below, and only with proof.

How to request a refund: Record a continuous, unedited video of yourself opening the sealed package for the first time, showing the shipping label and the bill stuck onto the box clearly in the video before it's opened. Send this video to us via WhatsApp or email (see Contact Us) along with your Order ID. We will only consider a refund after reviewing this proof and confirming the issue with it ourselves.

Deadline to submit proof: The unboxing video must be sent to us within 24 hours of the order being marked delivered. Proof submitted after this 24-hour window will not be accepted and the order will not be eligible for a refund.

Refunds are given only for:

  • A completely wrong colour sent (not a slight shade difference — see below).
  • A completely wrong product sent (a different item than what you ordered).
  • A genuinely damaged product on arrival.

Refunds are not given for:

  • Slight colour variation between the product received and the website photos — this can happen due to photography lighting and screen display differences, and is not treated as a wrong colour.
  • Small, borderline holes on handloom sarees — this is a natural characteristic of handloom weaving, not a defect.
  • Slightly uneven prints on hand block-printed sarees — this is a natural characteristic of hand printing, not a defect.

Submitting proof does not automatically guarantee a refund. Whether the proof genuinely supports one of the eligible cases above is assessed by us, and the final decision to approve or decline a refund rests entirely with Ankitha Vastralaya.

Once we've reviewed your proof and approved the refund, it will be processed within 10 days back to your original payment method (the card, UPI, or other method you paid with via Razorpay).

Missed delivery attempts: If a delivery attempt is made and no one is available to collect the parcel, it is the customer's responsibility to arrange for someone else to collect it on their behalf, or to coordinate a re-attempt with the courier directly. Ankitha Vastralaya is not responsible for delays, additional charges, or return-to-origin of the parcel caused by a missed delivery attempt, and no refund will be issued on that basis alone.

Order cancellations: Once an order is placed and payment is confirmed, it cannot be cancelled from the customer's end, under any circumstances. Ankitha Vastralaya reserves the right to cancel any order from its own end, at any time and for any reason, in which case a full refund will be issued to the original payment method.
